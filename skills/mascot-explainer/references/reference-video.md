# Reference video breakdown (1/2): overview and 0:00-4:15

The analysed reference: an 8:25 calisthenics explainer (1920×1080, 30 fps, about 1,260 narrated words, music bed
and SFX). Flat vector cartoon, thick black outlines, one muscular yellow mascot, a violet palette of 4-5 shades with
white sparkles, bold italic condensed headlines. Text only: no frame of it is in this repo. Brand, app and book names
are replaced. Built from 3 fps frame sheets per 85 s segment, so boundaries are good to about ±0.3 s.

## How it is made, in numbers
- 143 visual beats; median beat 3.0 s; 17 beats per minute.
- Frame-by-frame figure loops are on screen about 32 % of the time (exercise demos, looping
  figures inside bubbles); everything else is still art moved by the camera and motion graphics.
- Per-second frame difference is near zero on holds and spikes on transitions: most of the time the picture is a
  held composition with small idle motion (float, twinkle, slow push).

## Beats no template covers (and the closest one)
- Astronaut close-ups, rocket-on-ground scene and the figure-with-raised-finger torso close-up (flat lavender bg plus character art, no template).
- "THE REAL PROBLEM" typed headline works as textSlam only if typing with a cursor is supported (letter-spacing contracts as it settles).
- Padlock lock/unlock toggles and "404" badges on discs (bubble overlays, not in bubbles template).
- Thought-bubble ring (white circle with dot trail) around a head, with exercise loops inside.
- Phone feed UI (hearts, comments, collections sheet, "Pause to find your fate" screen) and parallax planets behind the phone; flat-lavender cross-fade-to-black transitions.
- Hunched hero with phone and shield with crack lines (custom still + draw-on cracks); no template.
- Full-frame lens flare cross and spiral-ring inward travel through the window (a zoom-out variant of windowRoom).
- Hero vs silhouette hugging a pile of planets, with a burst of discs (custom compare, no template).
- Back-view seated silhouette in a gym room with drifting bubbles (custom still scene).
- Jar-to-planet morph and deep-purple background tint shift (shelf to bubbles hand-over).
- Banana confetti swarm and portrait hero shot (3:25-3:35): no template; needs a "confetti swarm" layer over a still portrait.
- Thought-bubble pop-ups with props near a bar (4:08-4:12) are only loosely like crowd; ink-burst line pop is a custom effect.
- Flame aura loops (3:02, 4:00) and pause freeze fit powerUp but the flame is a drawn asset behind frame-by-frame figures.
- Rocket scene variants with part-swapping (wings to boosters, porthole icon swaps): flyBy covers only path movement; needs a rocket-assembly variant.

Coverage: every second is in a row.
